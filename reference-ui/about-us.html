One partner, six services, one story.

Pixerio is a creative and digital services company in JC Nagar, Bengaluru. The range of work is real: identity, print, packaging, exhibition stands, websites, film, 3D and e-learning.

The position

Six services read as six vendors unless one partner connects them.

A developer looking for walkthroughs does not need LMS integration. An L&D lead does not need VFX. Each service has its own buyer, and each page is written for that buyer.

What connects them is the route from brand definition through to a launched, findable product — the same team, the same standards, one conversation.

How we work

Three stages, in this order

Discovery to deployment is a real process here, and it is kept. What changes is that each stage ends in something you can hold.

01

Discovery

What the business needs the work to do, who it is for, and what already exists. Scope, sequence and the decisions that need a client in the room.

Output: a written scope
02

Implementation

Design and build against that scope. Reviews at fixed points, not at the end, so a change of mind costs a conversation rather than a rebuild.

Output: approved work
03

Deployment

Launch, handover and documentation. Files, access and the maintenance position all written down before anyone moves on to the next project.

Output: a documented handover
