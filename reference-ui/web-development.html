Questions we get asked

Straight answers

The questions buyers ask before they make contact, answered without a sales line in front of them.

What does a web development company actually do?

Designs and builds the site or application, then maintains it: structure, interface, front end, back end, integrations and the hosting it runs on.

How is website development costed?

Per project, after the scope is agreed. Pages, integrations and content migration drive the number far more than visual design does.

Why would we rebuild the current site?

When the current one cannot do a job the business now needs: mobile behaviour, enquiry handling, integration, or speed. We measure before recommending it.

Can you take over an existing site?

Yes. The first step is an audit of what exists, including the URLs and the search history behind them.
