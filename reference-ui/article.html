Why most briefs produce a price instead of an answer

A brief that names a deliverable gets a quote. A brief that names an outcome gets a conversation about the right deliverable. The difference is five sentences.

The article template carries a heading ladder, one pull quote, one figure with a caption, and a closing action. Body copy is written per article; the structure does not change.

Template note

Article body, author, date and reading time come from the content calendar. Nothing is back-dated or pre-filled.
