Questions we get asked

Straight answers

The questions buyers ask before they make contact, answered without a sales line in front of them.

How many revisions do we get?

Two rounds of revision are standard on identity work, and the scope states them. Further rounds are quoted rather than absorbed silently.

Who owns the files at the end?

The client, on final payment. Source files and the working assets are handed over, not withheld.

What if we do not like the first direction?

That is what the first review is for. A direction is presented with the reasoning, so the feedback is about the reason and not only the look.

How are the files delivered?

A shared folder with print-ready and screen-ready versions, plus the source files and a short note on what each one is for.
